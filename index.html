Government subsidy available

Flexible Financing

Finance it through PM Surya Ghar Yojana

Beyond the ₹78,000 subsidy, you can apply for a collateral-free solar loan through the government's Jan Samarth portal — with public-sector banks offering rates around 7-7.5% and tenures up to 10 years. The subsidy is credited directly into your loan account, lowering what you owe. We guide you through the entire application.
